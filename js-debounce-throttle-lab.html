What to notice

  • Debounce waits until no new event arrives for the selected delay. It is useful for search suggestions and autosave.
  • Throttle limits how frequently a callback runs during a stream of events. It is useful for scroll and resize work.
  • Try a short delay, then a longer one. Pause between words to see the debounced callback fire.
